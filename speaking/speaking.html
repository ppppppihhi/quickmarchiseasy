🎤 SPEAKING

Look at the questions and practise speaking.

PART 1: QUESTIONS

Answer the questions orally.

Question 1
How did you come to school today?
Hôm nay em đi học bằng phương tiện gì?
Question 2
What subjects did you have yesterday?
Hôm qua em đã học những môn gì?
Question 3
What is the biggest shop near your house?
Cửa hàng lớn nhất gần nhà em là cửa hàng nào?
Question 4
When are you going to have your next English lesson?
Khi nào em sẽ có tiết học tiếng Anh tiếp theo?
Question 5
Who are you going to see this evening?
Tối nay em dự định gặp ai?
Question 6
Who cooks the best food in your family?
Ai là người nấu ăn ngon nhất trong gia đình em?
Question 7
What was the weather like yesterday?
Thời tiết ngày hôm qua như thế nào?
Question 8
What is your favourite sport?
Môn thể thao yêu thích của em là gì?
Question 9
How much homework did you do yesterday?
Hôm qua em đã làm bao nhiêu bài tập về nhà?
Question 10
What are you going to do this weekend?
Em dự định làm gì vào cuối tuần này?
Question 11
How many shops are there near your house?
Có bao nhiêu cửa hàng ở gần nhà em?
Question 12
How many times a day do you clean your teeth?
Em đánh răng mấy lần một ngày?
Question 13
Where did you go for your last holiday?
Kỳ nghỉ vừa qua em đã đi đâu?
Question 14
Who is the best teacher in your school?
Ai là giáo viên giỏi nhất ở trường em?
Question 15
When were you born?
Em sinh vào khi nào?

PART 2: TOPIC CARDS

Use the prompts to practise speaking.

Topic 1: My Last Holiday

For my last holiday, I went to (1) ____________________ [Carlin Lake/the beach/the country]. I went there with (2) ____________________ [my family/my friends]. The weather was (3) ____________________ [beautiful/sunny/hot]. In the morning, I (4) ____________________ [swam/had sailing lessons]. We also (5) ____________________ [caught some fish/ate at a restaurant]. I felt very (6) ____________________ [happy/excited] because it was a wonderful trip.

Topic 2: My Last School Trip

On my last school trip, I went to (1) ____________________ [a museum/the zoo/a hall]. I went there by (2) ____________________ [school bus/train]. My (3) ____________________ [teacher and classmates] went with me. We saw (4) ____________________ [a puppet show/some animals]. We (5) ____________________ [ate a picnic/took photos]. The trip was (6) ____________________ [really interesting/fun].

Topic 3: Last Weekend

Last weekend, I stayed (1) ____________________ [at home/at a friend’s house]. On Saturday, I (2) ____________________ [played football/read in bed]. On Sunday, the weather was (3) ____________________ [rainy/cloudy/sunny]. I (4) ____________________ [did my homework/watched TV]. I also (5) ____________________ [went to a singing competition/saw my cousins]. It was a (6) ____________________ [quiet/busy] weekend.

Topic 4: My Last Birthday

On my last birthday, I was (1) ____________________ [ten/eleven] years old. I had a party at (2) ____________________ [my house/a restaurant]. I invited (3) ____________________ [my best friends/my family]. We ate (4) ____________________ [cake/pizza] and drank (5) ____________________ [juice/lemonade]. My parents gave me (6) ____________________ [a big present/a guitar]. I was very happy!

Topic 5: My Favourite Time of Day

My favourite time of day is the (1) ____________________ [morning/afternoon/evening]. I like it because I (2) ____________________ [can play sports/watch a movie]. I usually (3) ____________________ [have dinner/clean my teeth] with (4) ____________________ [my parents/my brother]. It is a (5) ____________________ [quiet/exciting] time for me. I feel (6) ____________________ [relaxed/good].
